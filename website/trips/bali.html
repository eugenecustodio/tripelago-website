About this trip

We base this trip in Ubud rather than the busy south, so mornings start with birdsong over the rice fields. Days alternate between temples, waterfalls and workshops, with a final stretch on the coast.

Highlights

  • Tegallalang rice terraces at sunrise
  • Tirta Empul water temple
  • Batik or silver-making workshop
  • Uluwatu temple and kecak dance
  • Beach day in Canggu

How many highlights you visit depends on your plan. The sample itinerary below updates when you change plans.

Sample itinerary (5 days, 4 nights)

  1. Day 1Arrive in Bali. Your transfer takes you to the hotel, then an evening welcome walk with your guide.
  2. Day 2Tegallalang rice terraces at sunrise.
  3. Day 3Tirta Empul water temple.
  4. Day 4Batik or silver-making workshop.
  5. Day 5Slow morning, then your transfer to the airport.
