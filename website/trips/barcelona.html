About this trip

Barcelona mixes beach, architecture and food like few cities can. We cover the Gaudí landmarks with skip-the-line access, then leave the afternoons for the Gothic Quarter and the evenings for tapas crawls.

Highlights

  • Sagrada Família with early entry
  • Park Güell and Casa Batlló
  • Tapas crawl in El Born
  • Montserrat day trip
  • Beach afternoon in Barceloneta
