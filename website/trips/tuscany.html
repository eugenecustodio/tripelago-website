About this trip

This is a trip for people who like to eat. Based in a countryside agriturismo, we visit Florence, Siena and San Gimignano, learn to make pasta, and spend a day among the vineyards of Chianti.

Highlights

  • Florence's Uffizi and the Duomo climb
  • Siena and the Piazza del Campo
  • Pasta-making in a farmhouse kitchen
  • Chianti wine route by van
  • Sunset in San Gimignano

How many highlights you visit depends on your plan. The sample itinerary below updates when you change plans.
