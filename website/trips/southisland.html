About this trip

From Queenstown we loop through the country's most dramatic landscapes: Milford Sound, the lakes of Wanaka and Tekapo, and the Southern Alps. Plenty of short hikes, and a few optional adrenaline stops.

Highlights

  • Milford Sound cruise
  • Lake Wanaka and Roys Peak walk
  • Stargazing at Lake Tekapo
  • Queenstown gondola and lakeside dinner
  • Mount Cook glacier valley hike

How many highlights you visit depends on your plan. The sample itinerary below updates when you change plans.

  2. Day 2Milford Sound cruise.
  3. Day 3Lake Wanaka and Roys Peak walk.
