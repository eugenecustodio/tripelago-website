FAQ

Can't find what you need? Message a trip specialist and we'll reply within one business day.

How do I book a trip?

Pick a plan (Wander, Discover or Explore More), choose a destination and departure date, add it to your cart and check out. You get a booking reference straight away, and a trip specialist replies within one business day to confirm your seats.

Do I pay on the website?

No payment is taken on the site. Within one business day of your order, a trip specialist emails payment instructions for the method you chose (GCash, card link or bank transfer). Your seats are held for 2 business days after that email.

Is airfare included?

Our prices cover accommodation, guided experiences and the transfers listed in your plan. International and domestic flights are not included, but our trip specialists can book them for you at cost.

How does the group discount work?

Book three or more travelers on the same destination, plan and departure date and 10% is taken off that trip automatically. It can be combined with one promo code. FIRSTTRIP gives 5% off a first booking.

What is the maximum group size?

Twelve. Every departure is capped at 12 travelers so the experience stays personal and guides can adapt the day to the group. The cart won't let you add more than 12 travelers to one destination on one date.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Changes to dates or plan are free up to 30 days before departure. Cancellations more than 30 days out are refunded in full minus a ₱2,000 processing fee per person; within 30 days, 50% is refundable; within 7 days, bookings are non-refundable. You can request a change from your account page.

Is travel insurance included?

Basic travel insurance is included on Discover and Explore More. On Wander trips you can add it for ₱1,200 per traveler, either on the trip page or in your cart.

Can I save trips for later?

Yes. Tap the heart on any destination to save it. Saved trips appear under Saved on the Destinations page and in your account.

Do you help with visas?

Yes. Once you book, your trip specialist sends a visa checklist for your destination and the booking documents most embassies ask for.
