1. Booking and payment

A booking is confirmed when payment is received. Within one business day of your order we email payment instructions, and seats are held for 2 business days after that email; unpaid seats are released after that. Prices are quoted in Philippine pesos per person and are the price shown for your destination and plan, per person on a twin-share basis. Airfare, visa fees and personal expenses are not included unless stated.

2. Changes and cancellations

Date or plan changes are free up to 30 days before departure, subject to availability. Cancellations more than 30 days before departure are refunded in full less a ₱2,000 processing fee per person. Cancellations within 30 days are 50% refundable; within 7 days, non-refundable. If Tripelago cancels a departure, you receive a full refund or a free transfer to another date.

3. Discounts and promo codes

The group discount (10% off a trip with 3 or more travelers on the same destination, plan and date) is applied automatically. One promo code may be used per order and is applied after the group discount; promo codes do not apply to travel insurance. Plan deals (such as ESCAPE8, DUO2000, GOFAR7) only reduce trips booked on the plan they name. Discounts cannot be exchanged for cash. Each departure takes at most 12 travelers.

4. Travel documents and insurance

Travelers are responsible for holding a valid passport and the visas required for their destination. Basic travel insurance is included on Discover and Explore More plans. Wander travelers may add it for ₱1,200 per person on the trip page or in the cart. We strongly recommend insurance for every trip.

5. Privacy

We collect only the details needed to book and operate your trip: your name, contact details, traveler information and payment confirmations. We share them with hotels, guides and transport partners for your booking and never sell them. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for one year. You may ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to hello@tripelago.com.

6. About this website

Your cart, saved trips, account and booking history are stored in your own browser, so they stay on the device you used. Clearing your browser data removes them. No payment details are collected on the site.
